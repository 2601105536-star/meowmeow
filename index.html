WELCOME TO MEOW CAFE

Your purrrfect place to be.

Mission

We are so in love with cats and we wanted to share our experience of how it is like to be a cat person for a day. We at Meow Cafe has created a safe haven for cat lovers and the likes. We want them to relax, have fun, and celebrate a purrfect kind of love for cats for at least a day in their lives. We hope to educate people with responsible pet ownership especially for cats. We also want to promote foster care for stray cats as part of our Corporate Social Responsibility.

Vision

Cats are an epitome for self-love. They always put themselves at first. They know how to relax and when to have fun. And with all of these factors, we came up with the idea of putting up the first-ever CAT Cafe in Bukidnon not to forget Tomas, our British Short hair cat. We believe in a holistic way to spend time with cats that would help you relax as you spend time with them. Plus adding up our must-try dishes and savory drinks. We at Meow Cafe aim to reach out to Cat Lovers from Northern Mindanao and nearby provinces and cities. It is ambitious but somehow we hope to branch out in other places hopefully. Our diversified culture perfectly represents that animals and humans can perfectly co-exist.
